Ixora — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

Development environment

This policy describes what the Ixora app collects and how it is used. It reflects the app's current, actual behavior — it does not describe planned or hypothetical features. Ixora is currently in active development; this document will be revised as functionality changes.

Who we are

Ixora is a mobile application for creating and running ambient "Vibes" — combinations of audio and, optionally, smart home device actions — started manually or on a schedule.

Account information

Ixora uses Firebase Authentication to sign you in. We store your name, email address, a Firebase-issued identifier, your timezone, and optionally an avatar image URL, in order to operate your account.

Push notifications

If you enable notifications, we store a device push token (issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging), your device platform and model, and app version, so we can deliver notifications about your schedules and smart home actions to your device. Notification content is limited to what is needed to identify what happened and route you to the right screen when tapped — it does not include credentials or sensitive device data.

Smart home connections

If you connect a smart home provider (for example, Home Assistant), we store the connection credentials you provide, encrypted, and use them only to read your devices' status and execute the actions you configure. We store basic device information (name, type, and status) needed to let you build Vibes and automations around your devices.

Google Home

Ixora is developing support for connecting your Google Home. This feature is not yet publicly available. When you authorize it, account selection, structure selection, and permission consent all happen within Google's own consent screens — Ixora only receives access to what you explicitly grant, and only for the device types and structures you approve.

We do not sell, share, or use Google Home data for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI or machine learning models. Device identifiers and state obtained through Google Home are retained only for as long as necessary to provide the feature you configured, in line with Google's Developer Policies for the Home APIs. You can revoke Ixora's access to your Google Home at any time from your Google Account settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with third parties for advertising.
  • We do not use your smart home or Google Home data to train AI models.

Third-party services we use to operate the app

Firebase (authentication and push notification delivery), and, when applicable, the smart home providers you explicitly connect (for example Home Assistant, or Google Home once available). Their own privacy terms apply to their part of the data flow.

Contact

For privacy questions about this development environment, contact the Ixora team through the project's GitHub repositories.
